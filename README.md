### 🔍 What I Do

- 🧲 **Talent Acquisition** – End-to-end recruitment: sourcing, screening, interviewing, offer management and closing.
- 🛠️ **Technical & Non-Technical Hiring** – Hiring across engineering, IT, operations, sales and support functions.
- 🏢 **HR Operations** – Employee records, HR documentation, policy implementation and process improvement.
- 🤝 **Employee Relations** – Resolving concerns and building a culture of trust and transparency.
- 💰 **Payroll Coordination** – Attendance, leave and data inputs for accurate, on-time payroll.
- 📈 **Performance Management** – Goal setting, review cycles and feedback support.
- 📊 **HR Analytics** – Turning attrition, hiring and engagement data into actionable insights.
- 🎉 **Employee Engagement** – Initiatives that boost morale, retention and belonging.
- ⚖️ **HR Compliance** – Ensuring adherence to labour laws and company policies.
- 🚪 **Onboarding & Offboarding** – Smooth, welcoming joinings and respectful, well-managed exits.
